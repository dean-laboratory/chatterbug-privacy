# Chatterbug Privacy Policy

- Effective date: November 26, 2026
- Applies to: Chatterbug for Android, version 1.0.0 and later

○○○ ("we") cares about the privacy of the parents and children who use the Chatterbug app (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it keeps it, and how it is used.

## 1. Summary

- We do not run any server, and we do not collect personal information from the people who use the app.
- Learning records are stored on your device. Only if you connect Google Drive are they also stored in an app-only area of your own Google Drive. We cannot see these records.
- The app has no advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools.
- The app does not use the microphone and does not record or store your child's voice or photos.

## 2. Information the app handles

### 2.1 Information stored on your device

To continue learning where you left off, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access this storage.

| Information | Contents | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning records | English sentences practiced, the grade the parent gave each sentence (Perfect, Good, Try again), and the dates of study | Choosing the next conversation and the sentences to review |
| Conversations | Prepared conversations and the position in the conversation in progress | Resuming a conversation |
| Settings | The dinosaur characters chosen by the parent and child, and the starting level | Matching the screens and the conversation level |
| Device identifier | A random value created when the app first runs | Telling records from different devices apart in Google Drive |

The device identifier is a random value. It is not related to any hardware serial number, advertising ID or phone number, and it does not identify a person.

The app does not ask for or store your child's name, age, photos, voice or location.

### 2.2 Information stored in Google Drive

Only when you choose "Google Drive 연결" (Connect Google Drive) in the app, the information in 2.1 is stored in your own Google Drive.

- The app requests access only to the app-only area that Google provides (appDataFolder, the `drive.appdata` scope). With this access the app cannot see or change any other files or folders in your Drive.
- Files in the app-only area do not appear in your Drive and cannot be read by other apps.
- The app does not receive the name, email address or profile photo of your Google account.
- The access granted by Google is used only on your device to read and write the app's Drive files. It is never sent to us.
- Information in Google Drive is kept by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

All learning features work without connecting Google Drive. In that case, records stay only on that device.

### 2.3 Reading sentences aloud

When you tap the speaker button, or when a new sentence appears in a conversation, the app sends the English sentence to the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your device to read it aloud. Only the English sentence shown on screen is sent, and the engine is the one chosen by your device maker or by you. How that engine handles information follows its provider's policy.

## 3. Sharing and processors

Because we do not collect your information, we do not share it with third parties or ask anyone to process it for us. When you connect Google Drive, information moves only between your device and your own Google account, as you choose.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in the device settings.
- Information in Google Drive is kept until you delete it. To delete it:
  1. Open Google Drive on a computer and go to Settings, then "Manage apps".
  2. Under Chatterbug, open "Options" and choose "Delete hidden app data".
- To revoke the app's access to Drive, go to "Security" in your Google Account, open "Third-party apps & services", and remove Chatterbug.

## 5. Children's privacy

A parent installs and sets up the app, and the child uses it together with the parent. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by the laws of your country), and it has no screen where a child enters information. The app has no advertising and no links that take the user outside the app.

## 6. Your rights

All records exist only on your device and in your own Google Drive, so you can delete them at any time as described in section 4. Because we do not hold your records, we cannot access, correct or delete them on your behalf. If you have questions, please contact us at the address in section 7.
